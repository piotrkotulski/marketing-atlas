# Partnerships and PR

Borrow audiences before you buy them.

## When to use

Read `.agents/marketing-context.md`. Match scope to stage — early stage relies on partnerships more than mature brands do.

## Core frameworks

### Co-marketing partners worth pursuing
Three filters:
- **Same audience, different product** (not competitors)
- **Similar size and credibility** (asymmetric partnerships usually fail)
- **Complementary value** (joint webinar, joint guide, joint event makes obvious sense)

### Partnership formats by effort
- **Low**: cross-newsletter mentions, joint social posts, podcast guest swaps
- **Medium**: joint webinar, co-authored content, bundle offer
- **High**: integrated product, co-built tool, joint conference, joint launch

Start low. Many low-effort partnerships beat one heroic project.

### Affiliate / referral programs
- Pick a commission that survives margins (10–30% one-time or 10–20% recurring is typical)
- Make the tracking trivial (UTM + dedicated link)
- Brief affiliates with creative they can use
- Pay on time

### PR that works in 2026
- "Story angles" beat press releases
- Tier journalists by beat (who covers your space)
- Pitches: one paragraph, one story, one expert (you) available
- Embargo for big launches; exclusives for biggest titles
- Build relationships before you need them

### Influencer collaborations
- Micro (5k–50k) often outperforms macro on ROI for niche products
- Pick by audience overlap, not follower count
- Brief on outcome and constraints; let them write in their voice
- Disclosure is non-negotiable (legal and trust)
- Measure: discount code use, UTM, direct attribution + brand lift

### Community marketing
- Pick one platform (Slack/Discord/FB Group/Circle) and commit
- Show up daily for 90 days before judging
- Members > broadcast: ratio of community-generated to brand-generated content should grow over time

## Compliance and limits

- Disclose paid partnerships: #ad, #sponsored, #reklama, #współpraca (UOKiK guidance in Poland; FTC in US).
- Affiliate disclosures on content where affiliate links live.
- GDPR/RODO: joint controllership agreements when sharing data between partners.
- DSA: platforms must enable transparency on ad relationships.

## Polish market notes (active when Polish layer is enabled in calibrator)

- Polish stack defaults documented in `.agents/marketing-context-pl.md` (BLIK, InPost, Allegro, SALESmanago, GetResponse, Senuto, Brand24, Piwik PRO).
- Active regulations: Omnibus (lowest-price-from-30-days on every promotion), AI Act art. 50 (AI labeling from 2026-08-02), EAA/WCAG 2.2 (accessibility, since 2025-06-28), dyr. 2023/2673 (visible withdrawal button, no dark patterns, from 2026-06-19), GDPR/RODO baseline. Regulator: UOKiK and UODO.
- Polish consumers are research-driven and price-sensitive; social proof in Polish and trust signals (Sprawdzona Opinia, Ceneo reviews, BLIK + InPost as defaults) lift conversion materially.
