# Video script

The first three seconds decide everything.

## When to use

Read `.agents/marketing-context.md` first. Match language and tone.

## Core frameworks

### The 3-second rule
Three things must hit at second 0:
- **Visual hook** — what the eye sees
- **Verbal hook** — what the ear hears
- **Text overlay** — what the silent viewer reads

If any one is missing, retention drops fast.

### Short-form structures (15–60 seconds)
**Problem-solution**
[0-3s] State the problem
[3-10s] Why it matters
[10-25s] Solution / method
[25-30s] CTA

**List**
[0-3s] "X things that [outcome]"
[3-50s] Items, one every 5–8 seconds
[50-60s] CTA

**Tutorial**
[0-3s] Show the end result
[3-8s] "Here's how"
[8-50s] Steps, fast
[50-60s] Result + CTA

### Longer formats
- **YouTube (5–15 min)**: hook in 0–15s, payoff promise, deliver, then CTA. Pattern interrupts every 30–60s.
- **Live shopping**: open with a hook + price reveal moment within the first minute; have 3–5 micro-moments planned.

### Captions and subtitles
- Most viewers watch without sound
- Max 2 lines on screen, 3–5 words per line
- Bold sans-serif, high contrast, black outline
- Highlight 1–2 key words per line
- Match timing to speech exactly

### Common script mistakes
- Slow build-up (state the point now)
- No text on screen
- One static shot for 30 seconds
- "Hi, my name is…" intros — cut them
- Reading the on-screen text out loud — say something *different* than what's on screen

## Compliance and limits

- AI Act art. 50: AI-generated voice, face, or content must be labeled; deepfakes of real people require disclosure regardless of consent.
- Disclose paid partnerships and ads (FTC in US; UOKiK in PL — #reklama or #współpraca clearly visible).
- Music licensing for any platform that doesn't provide cleared library.
- Accessibility: captions are required for most contexts under EAA/WCAG 2.2 in EU.

## Questions to ask the user

1. Platform and length (15s TikTok? 60s Reel? 8-min YouTube?)
2. One thing the viewer should do after watching?
3. What can you show on camera (product, screen, talking head, demo)?
4. Hook — do you have a single sentence or visual that earns the next 5 seconds?
