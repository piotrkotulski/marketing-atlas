# Social presence

Show up where the audience already is, with a reason to stop scrolling.

## When to use

Read `.agents/marketing-context.md`. Platform mix depends on market and audience.

## Core frameworks

### Platform fit (general guide)
- **LinkedIn** — B2B, professional, hiring; long-form posts and carousels work
- **Twitter/X** — tech, media, fast cycles; threads and reactions
- **Instagram** — visual brands, lifestyle, fashion, food; Reels dominant
- **TikTok** — broad reach, younger; raw and authentic outperforms polished
- **YouTube** — long-form trust + Shorts for discovery; the only platform where evergreen still pays
- **Facebook** — communities and Groups; underrated for SMB and local

### Personal brand vs company brand
- Personal accounts get 5–10× the reach of company accounts on most platforms
- For B2B founders and consultants: personal is the primary, company is secondary
- For consumer brands: company is primary, founder optional

### Content pillars (3–5)
Borrow the framework from `content-engine`. Same pillars, platform-specific format.

### Hooks (first line decides)
- Curiosity: "I was wrong about [X]."
- Story: "Last week, [unexpected]."
- Value: "How to [outcome] without [pain]."
- Contrarian: "Unpopular opinion: [bold]."

### Cadence that works
- LinkedIn — 3–5×/week is sustainable and effective
- Twitter/X — daily, with at least one thread per week
- Instagram — 1 post + Stories daily for visual brands; Reels 3–5×/week
- TikTok — 1–4×/day if you can; less, and the algorithm forgets you
- YouTube — 1 long-form/week + 2–4 Shorts/week

Volume rarely beats consistency. Pick a cadence you can hold for 3 months.

### Repurposing — one piece, many surfaces
See `content-engine` for the atoms-per-piece framework.

### Engagement is content
30 minutes a day: reply to comments, comment on 5–10 posts in your space, message 2–3 people. Compound over months.

## Compliance and limits

- AI Act art. 50 (from 2026-08-02): label AI-generated or AI-edited content.
- Disclosure of paid partnerships (#ad, #sponsored; #reklama / #współpraca in Poland — UOKiK guidance for influencers).
- DSA: platforms must disclose ad reach; brands should not hide partnerships.
- GDPR/RODO: lead capture from social requires consent and information.
- Don't share medical, financial, or legal advice you're not qualified to give.

## Polish market notes (active when Polish layer is enabled in calibrator)

- Polish stack defaults documented in `.agents/marketing-context-pl.md` (BLIK, InPost, Allegro, SALESmanago, GetResponse, Senuto, Brand24, Piwik PRO).
- Active regulations: Omnibus (lowest-price-from-30-days on every promotion), AI Act art. 50 (AI labeling from 2026-08-02), EAA/WCAG 2.2 (accessibility, since 2025-06-28), dyr. 2023/2673 (visible withdrawal button, no dark patterns, from 2026-06-19), GDPR/RODO baseline. Regulator: UOKiK and UODO.
- Polish consumers are research-driven and price-sensitive; social proof in Polish and trust signals (Sprawdzona Opinia, Ceneo reviews, BLIK + InPost as defaults) lift conversion materially.
