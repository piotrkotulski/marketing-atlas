# Copy craft

Words that make the next click obvious.

## When to use

Read `.agents/marketing-context.md` first. If brand-positioning has been run, use its messaging hierarchy as input. Match the user's language (PL/EN).

## Core frameworks

### Hierarchy on every page
- **Hook** (headline) — promise the outcome or name the problem
- **Subhead** — make the promise concrete
- **Proof** — numbers, names, quotes
- **Action** — one clear CTA

If a page has more than one primary CTA, it has zero primary CTA.

### Headline patterns that work
- **Outcome without pain** — "X without Y" ("Run payroll without spreadsheets")
- **For-the-right-person** — "[Category] for [specific person]" ("CRM for solo consultants")
- **Negation of the standard** — "Stop [common mistake]. [Better alternative]."
- **Number-driven** — "X things that [outcome]"
- **Question with a sharp answer underneath** — works only if the answer is *truly* sharp

### CTA rules
- Verb + what they get: "Get the checklist", "Book a demo", "Start free trial"
- Avoid "Submit", "Click here", "Continue" alone
- One primary CTA per page

### Voice rules
- Active voice
- Cut "very", "really", "actually", "just"
- Cut filler ("In today's fast-paced world…")
- Specific > vague: "save 4 hours a week" beats "save time"
- Read it aloud — if you trip, rewrite

### Anti-patterns (don't write like this)
- "Leverage", "synergize", "unlock the potential", "transformative", "holistic"
- "We are passionate about…" (everyone is; nobody cares)
- Exclamation marks (drop almost all of them)
- Stacked adjectives ("powerful, intuitive, scalable platform")

## Compliance and limits

- No false urgency or scarcity (UOKiK enforces; e.g., Beliani case in Poland).
- Substantiate claims (testimonials, statistics, certifications).
- Environmental and health claims: only with proof (EmpCo from Sep 2026 in EU).
- Regulated industries (finance, health): respect sector-specific disclosure rules. Polish mortgage broker example: RRSO and representative example must accompany any cost claim (art. 7–8 ustawy o kredycie hipotecznym, KNF supervision).
- AI Act art. 50 (from 2026-08-02): if AI generates copy live for users, disclose the AI interaction.

## Polish market notes (active when Polish layer is enabled in calibrator)

- Polish stack defaults documented in `.agents/marketing-context-pl.md` (BLIK, InPost, Allegro, SALESmanago, GetResponse, Senuto, Brand24, Piwik PRO).
- Active regulations: Omnibus (lowest-price-from-30-days on every promotion), AI Act art. 50 (AI labeling from 2026-08-02), EAA/WCAG 2.2 (accessibility, since 2025-06-28), dyr. 2023/2673 (visible withdrawal button, no dark patterns, from 2026-06-19), GDPR/RODO baseline. Regulator: UOKiK and UODO.
- Polish consumers are research-driven and price-sensitive; social proof in Polish and trust signals (Sprawdzona Opinia, Ceneo reviews, BLIK + InPost as defaults) lift conversion materially.

## Questions to ask the user

1. Which page or asset, and what is the single action you want from the reader?
2. Who lands here and where did they come from (cold ad, search, email, referral)?
3. Which proof points are usable (numbers, names, quotes)?
4. Tone preference (professional, casual, playful, technical)?
