# Brand positioning

Make the position before the noise.

## When to use

Run when:
- Launching something new
- Re-launching or rebranding
- Copy or campaigns keep falling flat (often a positioning problem, not a copy problem)
- Sales team can't explain who you're for

Read `.agents/marketing-context.md` first. If audience-discovery and market-intel have been run, read their outputs.

## Core frameworks

### The 4-line positioning statement
1. **For** [target customer]
2. **Who** [has this problem or job]
3. **We are** [category]
4. **That** [unique benefit], **unlike** [main alternative], **because** [proof or mechanism].

If you can't fill all four with confidence, you have a research gap — run `audience-discovery` or `market-intel`.

### Value proposition stack
- **Headline benefit** (one outcome the customer most wants)
- **3 supporting benefits** (the why-it-works)
- **Proof** (numbers, names, certifications, case studies)
- **Risk reducers** (guarantee, free trial, refund, easy cancellation)

### Messaging hierarchy
- **One-liner** — what you say in a tweet or elevator
- **30-second pitch** — same idea with proof
- **Full story** — for landing pages, decks, longer content

The one-liner must be sayable out loud without sounding like marketing.

### Differentiators worth keeping
Three tests:
- **Important** — customers actually care
- **Distinct** — competitors can't honestly claim it
- **Provable** — you can back it up

If a differentiator fails any test, it's a buzzword.

## Compliance and limits

- Claims must be substantiated (EU consumer law; Polish UOKiK).
- Environmental claims: backed by evidence (EmpCo from Sep 2026 in EU).
- Don't make medical, financial, or safety claims without regulatory grounding.

## Polish market notes (active when Polish layer is enabled in calibrator)

- Polish stack defaults documented in `.agents/marketing-context-pl.md` (BLIK, InPost, Allegro, SALESmanago, GetResponse, Senuto, Brand24, Piwik PRO).
- Active regulations: Omnibus (lowest-price-from-30-days on every promotion), AI Act art. 50 (AI labeling from 2026-08-02), EAA/WCAG 2.2 (accessibility, since 2025-06-28), dyr. 2023/2673 (visible withdrawal button, no dark patterns, from 2026-06-19), GDPR/RODO baseline. Regulator: UOKiK and UODO.
- Polish consumers are research-driven and price-sensitive; social proof in Polish and trust signals (Sprawdzona Opinia, Ceneo reviews, BLIK + InPost as defaults) lift conversion materially.

## Questions to ask the user

1. Who is the target customer in one sentence?
2. What's the single biggest job they're hiring you to do?
3. Why would someone pick you over the next best alternative?
4. What proof do you have for that "why"?
